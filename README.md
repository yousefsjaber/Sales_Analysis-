📊 Sales Performance Analysis | Power BI
🧩 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance data to support data-driven business decisions.
Using Power BI, the dashboard tracks key sales KPIs, evaluates performance against targets, and identifies trends, profitability drivers, and pricing impacts across products, categories, customer segments, and discount levels.

The analysis covers sales data from 2023 to 2024 and provides actionable insights for sales optimization and profitability improvement.

🎯 Business Objectives

Monitor overall sales and profit performance

Compare actual sales vs target

Analyze sales trends over time (MoM & YoY)

Identify top and bottom-performing products

Evaluate profitability by category and customer segment

Assess the impact of discounts on sales volume and profit

📈 Analysis Highlights
🔹 Sales Performance

Sales peaked during Q2, driven by increased order volume.

Performance against sales targets showed a significant gap, highlighting growth opportunities.

🔹 Product Analysis

Top-performing products contribute the majority of revenue and profit.

Bottom-performing products show low profitability and require pricing or demand review.

🔹 Category Analysis

Office Supplies generated lower sales volume but higher profit margins.

Technology and Furniture drove higher revenue with relatively lower margins.

🔹 Customer Segment Analysis

Consumer segment generates the highest sales volume.

Corporate segment provides stronger profitability per order.

🔹 Discount Impact

Discounts above 20% increase sales volume but significantly reduce profit margin.

Optimal pricing requires balancing discount levels and profitability.
